The Fine Print

Service details, explained upfront

Know what's included, how long it takes, and what's covered before you book.

Display Repair

What's Included
  • Full diagnostic before replacement
  • Screen, digitizer & touch calibration
  • Post-repair quality check
Estimated Time

Most display replacements are completed in 45–90 minutes, depending on device model and part availability.

Important Notes

Original-quality and OEM-grade options are available; we'll explain the difference and pricing before starting.

Battery Replacement

What's Included
  • Battery health check
  • Safe removal of the old/swollen cell
  • Charge-cycle test after fitting
Estimated Time

Typically 30–60 minutes for most smartphones and smartwatches.

Important Notes

Swollen batteries are handled with care to avoid damage to the surrounding frame or display.

Charging Repair

What's Included
  • Port cleaning & connector inspection
  • Charging IC diagnosis
  • Port or flex cable replacement, if needed
Estimated Time

Simple port cleaning takes under 30 minutes; component-level charging repairs may take longer.

Important Notes

We test with the cable and adapter you normally use, since some faults are cable-specific.

Motherboard Repair

What's Included
  • Chip-level fault tracing
  • Micro-soldering & component replacement
  • Liquid-damage cleaning & corrosion removal
Estimated Time

Usually 1–3 days, since board-level faults need careful diagnosis and testing.

Important Notes

Some liquid-damage or short-circuit cases may not be recoverable — we'll always confirm feasibility before charging.

Software Services

What's Included
  • OS reinstall, updates & boot-loop fixes
  • Virus & malware cleanup
  • Data backup before any software work
Estimated Time

Most software fixes are completed the same day, within 1–3 hours.

Important Notes

We always recommend backing up personal data beforehand; we can help with this on request.

Warranty is available on most repairs and replacement parts. The warranty period depends on the type of repair or part used. We'll always explain the applicable warranty before starting the repair.
